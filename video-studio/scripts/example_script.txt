# The quiet weight
Nobody claps for the man who comes home.
Nobody sees him sit in the truck for five extra minutes,
gathering what's left of himself before he walks through that door.
[pause 1.2]
But his children are watching.
They're always watching.

# What they will remember
They won't remember the speech you gave them about hard work.
They'll remember that you got up.
Every morning.
Tired, sore, and unseen.
You got up.

That's the sermon.
That's the lesson.
Your life is the only lecture they will ever truly hear.
