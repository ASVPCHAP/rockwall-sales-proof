### How it becomes a script
1. I outline 10–14 chapters (2–3 min each) with a 30-second hook and a quiet ending, then you approve the outline.
2. I write the script in the narration format (one thought per line, blank line = pause). About 3,000 words for 25 minutes.
3. You edit it in your own voice. This step is what makes the channel original.
4. `eleven.py say` generates the voice and `build.py` assembles the video.
